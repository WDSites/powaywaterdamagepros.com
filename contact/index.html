What to say on the call

  • Where the water came from, and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms are wet and how long the water has been there.
  • Whether it is clean water, dirty water or sewage.
  • Whether anything electrical is wet or near the water.
  • Your street address and ZIP code, so we can confirm you are in range.
  • Whether you have homeowners or renters insurance, so we can talk about documentation.

Poway is also the center of our service area. We take calls across Poway, Escondido and Ramona.

Before you call

If you can do it safely, close the main water valve, keep everyone out of standing water near electricity and photograph the damage. If you smell gas or see sparking, leave and call 911 or your utility first.

If the water has stopped but the room is wet, start from water mitigation. If it is still running or standing, start from emergency water removal.

What happens after you call

  1. A dispatcher listens and asks the questions that matter most: source, depth, rooms, power and category of water.
  2. You hear immediate safety steps, such as shutting off the main valve and keeping out of standing water near electricity.
  3. A crew is arranged. We offer same-day response when available and we do not promise an arrival time.
  4. On arrival the crew assesses with moisture meters and writes down the scope.
  5. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and you decide whether to proceed.

What to have ready

It helps to know where the main water valve is, who your insurer is and whether you own or rent the property. If you rent, have your landlord or property manager's number ready as well, since they often need to be told in writing.

Have your address and a good callback number. Photos of the damage on your phone are useful, and you can take them while you wait.

For landlords and property managers

Property managers often call after hours for a tenant. Tell the dispatcher the unit, the source and whether neighboring units are affected, since water travels. Ask the crew for moisture readings and a written scope to share with your insurer.

We do not repair plumbing or roofs. If the source is still active, a plumber or roofer is needed alongside the drying.

Hours and response

The line is open every hour of every day. Daytime calls and 3 a.m. calls get the same attention. Same-day response is offered when available, and we do not promise an arrival time, because traffic, weather and the number of active jobs change it.

What we can promise is a clear conversation. You will know what to do next before you hang up.

Email and non-urgent questions

For non-urgent questions about services or documentation, email contact@powaywaterdamagepros.com. Please do not use email for active water, since the phone line is the quickest way to reach a dispatcher.

Directions and visiting

Our listed address is 12700 Stowe Dr, Poway, CA 92064. Jobs happen at your property, so there is rarely a reason to visit, and the dispatcher will tell you if that changes. If you plan to drop off paperwork, call first.

If you are trying to describe where your property is to a crew, give the nearest cross streets and anything that is hard to find, such as a gate code, a long driveway, a dirt road or a rear unit. That saves time when the crew is on the way.

After the job

When the work is done, we provide the moisture readings, photos and scope notes for your records. If your insurer has questions about the documents, call the line and we will explain what the numbers mean.

If something does not look right after the crew leaves, such as a smell returning or a stain reappearing, call again. A second visit to check readings costs less than a second repair.
