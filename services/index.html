How the stages fit together

A typical loss moves through the services in order. Standing water is removed first. Drying follows, supervised by moisture readings. Cleanup removes soaked or contaminated materials and sanitizes. Repair rebuilds the finishes. Mold remediation is added when moisture has been present long enough for growth.

Some losses use only one stage. A small supply line leak caught in an hour may only need drying and a patch. A flood or sewage backup uses nearly all of them.

Common combinations
SituationServices usually involved
Burst pipe, caught quicklyWater removal, mitigation, repair
Slow leak, found weeks laterMitigation, mold inspection, cleanup, repair
Storm water through a garage doorFlood restoration, cleanup, mitigation
Sewage backupFlood restoration, cleanup, mitigation, repair
Stain on a ceiling, source already fixedRepair, with moisture checks first
