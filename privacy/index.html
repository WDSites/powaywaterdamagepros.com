Privacy Policy

Effective 2026. This policy explains what happens when you visit this site or call our line.

What we collect

This website has no forms. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site. When you call, we collect the phone number you call from and the information you choose to tell us, such as your address, the type of damage and your insurance situation.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use call information to understand your situation, arrange service, follow up on your request and improve how the line is run. We keep it only as long as needed for those purposes.

Third-party content

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when the content loads. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@powaywaterdamagepros.com. California residents may have additional rights under state law, and we will respond to valid requests.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@powaywaterdamagepros.com or mailed to 12700 Stowe Dr, Poway, CA 92064. See also our terms and contact page.
